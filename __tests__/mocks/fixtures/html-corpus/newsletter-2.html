Field Notes on Urban Gardening

Hi friends, the tomatoes are finally done, the garlic is in the ground, and the community plot on Elm Street has a waiting list for the first time in its history. Here is what I learned this season.

Soil is everything. We tested every bed in March and found lead levels in two of them that were well above the safe threshold. Raised beds with imported soil fixed it, but it cost us a month.

Water timing matters more than water volume. Switching to early-morning drip irrigation cut our usage by a third and the plants looked better for it.
