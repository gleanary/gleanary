Rates, Rumours, and a Very Strange IPO

Good morning. This week the central bank did exactly what everyone expected, which somehow still moved markets more than anything else that happened. We will get to why in a moment.

First, a correction: last week I wrote that the bond auction was undersubscribed. It was oversubscribed. Several of you wrote in, and you were right. Thank you, and sorry.

1. The rate decision

Holding rates steady was priced in for weeks. What was not priced in was the language in the statement, which dropped the phrase "for an extended period" for the first time since 2023. Traders read that as a signal, and two-year yields jumped eleven basis points within the hour.

2. The strangest IPO of the year

A company that sells subscription boxes of artisanal ice went public on Thursday. It has never turned a profit, its product melts, and it closed its first day up forty percent. I have no explanation. I have read the prospectus twice and I still have no explanation.
